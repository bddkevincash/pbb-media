# Privacy policy - PBB rclone

Last updated: November 2, 2026

"PBB rclone" is a private Google Cloud app belonging to the owner of the Pit Box B.E.T.S. project. It is used only by
its owner, with his own Google account, to copy his own files between his Google Drive folder and his own automated
data jobs (GitHub Actions).

- **Who uses it:** only the owner. The app is not offered, sold or made available to anyone else.
- **What it accesses:** files in the owner's own Google Drive, to read and write the project's data files. It does not
  access anyone else's account or data.
- **What is collected:** nothing beyond those files. No personal information about other people is collected, stored,
  shared or sold, and nothing is used for advertising.
- **Where data goes:** the owner's own Google Drive and his own private GitHub repository only.
- **Revoking access:** the owner can remove the app's access at any time in his Google Account under
  Security > Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
